shield_lock PRIVACY POLICY

Your data, untouched.

We break things for a living, so we keep this site boring on purpose: no cookies, no analytics, no trackers, no accounts.

Last updated: 13 October 2026

bolt
TL;DR

ehax.in is a static website. We do not set cookies, run analytics, show ads, or ask you to sign in. The only personal information we receive is what you choose to send us, for example an email, a Discord message, or a recruitment form.

01
WHO WE ARE

Who we are

EHAX is the official cybersecurity team of Delhi Technological University (DTU), New Delhi, India. This policy covers the website at ehax.in and its pages, including the EHAX101 guide. In this policy, "EHAX", "we", and "us" mean the team; "you" means anyone visiting the site.

02
WHAT WE COLLECT

What we collect

Through this website: nothing directly. There are no forms, logins, comment boxes, cookies, local storage, fingerprinting, or analytics scripts on ehax.in. The interactive effects you see (particles, the council ring, the SOC floor) run entirely in your browser and send nothing back to us.

When you contact us. If you email us or message us on Discord, LinkedIn, or GitHub, we receive whatever you send, such as your name, email address or username, and the content of your message. We use it only to reply to you and to run team activities.

When you register for an event or recruitment. Registrations (such as recCTF) are collected through Google Forms, which is linked from this site but not hosted on it. Those forms may ask for details like your name, email, roll number, branch, and year. We use this information only to run that event or recruitment round: to contact participants, check eligibility, and announce results.

03
THIRD PARTIES

Services we rely on

Even a static site has to be served from somewhere. When you load a page, your browser talks to these services, which can see technical data such as your IP address, browser, and the time of the request. They process it under their own privacy policies, not ours.

01

GitHub Pages: hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitor IP addresses for security and operations. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

02

Google Fonts: typefaces and icons

Fonts and icons are loaded from Google's servers, which receive your IP address and browser details. See the Google Fonts privacy FAQ.

03

Outbound links

Links to Discord, GitHub, LinkedIn, Google Forms, Google Drive, and CTF platforms take you to services we don't control. Once you leave ehax.in, their privacy policies apply.

04
USE & SHARING

How we use and share it

  • We never sell personal information, and we don't use it for advertising.
  • Access is limited to the EHAX council members who run the relevant event or recruitment.
  • We may share event details (for example a participant list) with DTU faculty advisors or co-organisers when that is needed to run the event.
  • Public results, such as CTF scoreboards or announcements of who joined the team, may show names or team handles. Tell us if you'd prefer not to be named.
  • We disclose information if the law or DTU authorities require it.
05
PEOPLE ON THIS SITE

Members and faculty

The site shows the names, roles, photos, and profile links of our council members and faculty advisors, published with their consent to represent the team. Anyone listed can ask us to update or remove their details at any time by emailing us.

06
RETENTION

How long we keep it

Registration responses are kept for as long as the event or recruitment cycle needs them, and normally deleted within a year after it ends. Emails and messages stay in our inbox or Discord server until they are no longer needed. You can ask us to delete them sooner.

07
YOUR RIGHTS

Your rights

In line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you;
  • correct or update it;
  • delete it, or withdraw consent you gave earlier;
  • look into a concern or grievance about how we handled it.

Email us from the address you used to contact or register with us and we'll respond as soon as we can, normally within 30 days.

08
SECURITY & MINORS

Security and minors

The site is served over HTTPS, and we keep registration data in access-controlled accounts. No system is perfectly secure, but as a security team we take that one personally.

Our activities are aimed at university students. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18 without a parent or guardian's consent. If you think we have, email us and we'll delete it.

09
CHANGES & CONTACT

Changes and contact

If we change how the site handles information, for example by adding analytics, we'll update this page and the "Last updated" date above first. The full history of this page is public in our GitHub.
