WHAT IS A CTF

Capture The Flag

CTF stands for "Capture The Flag." Since cybersecurity can be theory-heavy, CTFs were created to gamify the learning experience. While understanding the underlying theory is essential, your primary growth will come from the hands on process of solving these challenges.

03
LEARNING STRATEGY

How You'll Actually Learn

In CTFs you learn by solving, you'll pick up new tools while working through challenges, not before. So just start: watch a tutorial, ask AI, and look for challenge writeups (a writeup is the solution someone publishes after solving a challenge, you'll also be required to write these for recCTF later).

If you don't know how to solve a challenge, read its writeup. If it mentions a tool you don't know about, learn how that tool works with a quick YouTube video, then come back and retry the challenge. You can also ask anything in the group, your seniors are always there to help. Don't be shy to ask or discuss; showing interest and actually going through problems is exactly what gets you noticed, and it earns brownie points for recruitment.
